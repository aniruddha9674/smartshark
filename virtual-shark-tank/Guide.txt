Investor Dashboard

1. Profile — Name, photo, bio, PAN-verified badge, investment preferences (sector, ticket size, geography, stage), portfolio summary

2. Feed — Explore stream of matched/relevant businesses, filterable (sector, location, ticket size, readiness score), sorted by match %

3. Notifications — New matches, messages, businesses updating profiles/scores, saved-business activity

4. History — Past viewed businesses, past matches, past conversations, interaction timeline

5. Settings — Account details, notification preferences, password/security, language toggle

6. Analysis — Aggregate insights: sector-wise distribution of matches, comparison view across shortlisted businesses, trend of readiness scores among followed businesses

7. Following — List of businesses the investor is tracking/shortlisted, quick-access cards with score + status updates

Business Owner Dashboard

1. Profile — Business name, sector, description, documents/verification badge, editable core + sector-specific fields

2. Business Analysis — Readiness score with SHAP breakdown, improvement suggestions, historical score trend, benchmark vs similar businesses

3. Notifications — New matches, messages, verification status updates, score changes

4. History — Past matches, past investor interactions, profile edit history

5. Feed — Ranked list of matched investors, match explanation per card, filter/sort

6. Settings — Account details, notification preferences, password/security, language toggle

7. Following — Investors the business owner is tracking/shortlisted, quick-access cards

Registration

Investor Side:

Name, phone number (OTP verified), email
PAN number (identity verification only, not accreditation)
Investment preferences: sector, ticket size range, geography, stage
Short bio/description
Verification: identity-level only (PAN + OTP) — no net-worth/accreditation check, disclosed clearly as platform scope

Business Side:

Name, phone number (OTP verified), email
Business name, sector, location
Udyam Registration Number (primary verification anchor)
GST Number (optional — not all small businesses have one)
Shop Act License (optional)
Photo upload of at least one supporting document (OCR pre-fills fields automatically)
Short business description (template-assisted)
Verification: tiered — "Basic" (Udyam only) vs "Verified" (Udyam + GST cross-checked); async processing, non-blocking
